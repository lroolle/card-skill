Your turn · 1 ask waits on you

Your agent answers with cards. You judge each one.

A skill for coding agents. The agent lays its answer out as a board: one claim per card, at most one ask. Your reply goes back as data, one line per card.

Install Source
  1. Click the tab "6 Choose". The desk zooms to the card that waits on you.
  2. Pick Kafka, not the suggestion. Card 7 was written for NATS, so it holds itself back.
  3. Press Send. The text you get is the whole reply, as the agent reads it.

The loop

3
1

The agent writes board.org

Plain Org mode. A heading is a claim. A drawer says what the card asks of you.

** Pick NATS unless replay matters
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: pick-queue
:ASK: choose
:END:
NATS meets both needs.

- [X] [[#nats]]
- [ ] [[#kafka]]
2

You judge on one page

The top line says whose turn it is. A yellow-red tab stands on each card that waits on you. Every card takes Keep, Drop, More and a reply.

 Choose    pick an option
 Approve   go or no-go
 Answer    your own words
 Do        a step only you can take
3

The agent reads your reply

One batch. An ask you did not touch comes back as untouched, never as consent.

#6 pick-queue choose  changed: kafka
#7 cutover    approve held: #6 changed
#3 nats       mark    more

What a card gives you

6
1

One claim, at most one ask

A card is the smallest thing you can judge and point at. "Card 12 is wrong" is a complete review comment.

2

You see whose turn it is

The tab color means one thing: this waits on you. The browser tab counts the asks, and the count drops as you answer.

3

A changed card says so

Each card has versions. A revised card is marked Changed and keeps what it said before. An answer to an old version is flagged, not reused.

4

An ask knows its premise

An approval that rests on a choice waits for the choice. Change the choice, and the approval is held until the agent asks again.

5

The evidence is in the card

A screenshot, lines 40 to 60 of a file, a drawing of the flow. They travel inside the one HTML file, with no network.

6

Plain Org underneath

board.org is an outline that Emacs folds and GitHub renders. The agent learns no new format, and neither do you.

Then ask for something that ends in a decision:

Compare three queues for our ingest service and let me pick. Answer with cards.

The skill tells the agent when a board is earned and when prose is better. Most answers should stay prose.

Node 18 or later. No dependencies. The copy does not update itself: copy it again after a pull. cards --version says which build you have.

What it does not do

3
  • It is not for one answer. A board for one paragraph is costume. Use it for several judgments, a decision only you can make, or work that spans turns.
  • It is checked in Chromium only. Safari, Firefox and a phone in a hand are not verified yet. The list is in STATUS.md.
  • It runs no service. A board is a file on your disk. A published board is a static page; a reader copies the reply and sends it to you.
